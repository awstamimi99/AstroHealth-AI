Logo

Use .logo in HTML (sidebar, headers) or assets/brand/logo-full.svg as an image. Never use a NASA logo or insignia.

AstroHealth AICrew Health System

Colour tokens

Red is brand & interaction only — it never means a health state. Critical is a separate raspberry-magenta and always has its icon and label. A status is always colour + icon + text. The scan-* colours are for the dark body-scan stage only.

Typography

Inter for UI, JetBrains Mono for codes, IDs and timestamps. Numbers are tabular (0123456789).

Body scan stage, pins, rings & drawer

From js/components/body-scan.js: createBodyScan({ crop, pins, hotspots, onPinClick }), createRing() and scanStage(); pop-ups and drawers from openDialog() in js/components/dialog.js. The body is decorative (aria-hidden). Pins are ≥ 44 px buttons and must also be listed as text. Ring fill is a display scale only, never a status. Colours come only from the --color-scan-* tokens.

Icons

Usage: icon("name") from js/components/icon.js, or <i data-icon="name"></i> in static HTML. Need another? Add it to scripts/build-icons.mjs.
