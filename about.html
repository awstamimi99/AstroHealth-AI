About AstroHealth AI

AstroHealth AI is a research and educational prototype. It explores how a crew on a communication-limited space mission and a Medical Officer could review health records together, using simple rules that can always be explained.

All health, crew, mission and simulation data is synthetic. This is not a medical device, it is not clinically validated, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

What the prototype implements

  • Two roles: astronaut and Medical Officer, each with its own dashboard.
  • Physical health: synthetic heart rate, SpO₂, blood pressure and sleep readings with trends and data-quality labels.
  • Mental wellbeing: daily check-ins that are private unless the astronaut chooses to share them.
  • Simulation Center: deterministic, labelled synthetic scenarios (for example a low-oxygen demonstration) for the Medical Officer.
  • Alerts: created by four deterministic demonstration rules, acknowledged and resolved by the Medical Officer, with a full audit trail.
  • Health history, printable reports and settings built from the stored synthetic records.

How alerts work

Alerts come from deterministic, rule-based logic — no machine learning. Four demonstration rules compare stored readings (heart rate, SpO₂, systolic blood pressure, sleep duration) with stored demonstration thresholds that are not clinically validated. Rules run only after a simulation step or when the Medical Officer chooses to evaluate; nothing runs in the background. Every alert shows which rule fired, the readings used, when they were recorded, whether they were simulated, and why its severity was chosen. Suspect, invalid and stale readings are never used as evidence.

Privacy model

  • Astronauts see only their own records.
  • The Medical Officer never sees the private journal or the scores of check-ins an astronaut keeps private.
  • Medical Officer notes on alerts are internal and are not shown to astronauts.
  • Pages never show internal record identifiers, passwords or session details.
  • Sign-in is a local demonstration only. Demo accounts use the password demo; this is not real security.

Online and offline

All records are stored in this browser (IndexedDB) on this device. After the first successful visit, a service worker caches the app's own files so it keeps working without a network. The app loads no external fonts, scripts or analytics. Data is not synchronised between devices.

Medical limitations

AstroHealth AI does not diagnose, does not recommend treatment, and does not determine fitness for duty. Alerts and reports describe synthetic readings against demonstration rules only. Any real health decision belongs to qualified people following approved protocols.
