01 · Mission overview

When help is minutes away, the crew needs answers now.

A crew may be minutes or hours from Earth support. AstroHealth AI explores how stored readings, wellbeing check-ins and simple, explainable rules could help a crew and a Medical Officer see what changed and review it together. Everything in this prototype uses synthetic data.

⏱

Review

See each alert with its rule, the readings behind it and its full audit trail in one place.

☾

Sleep

Nightly sleep duration compared with a stored demonstration threshold over consecutive nights.

❤

Vital signs

Heart rate, SpO₂ and blood pressure readings with trends and clear data-quality labels.

🧠

Wellbeing check-ins

Private by default: only check-ins an astronaut chooses to share are visible to the Medical Officer.

02 · Core capabilities

Everything runs on this device.

❤

Physical Health Monitoring

Review synthetic heart rate, SpO₂, blood pressure and sleep readings with 7- and 30-day trends.

🧠

Mental Wellbeing Monitoring

Daily check-ins with explicit sharing — private check-ins and the private journal stay with the astronaut.

⚙

Explainable Demonstration Rules

Four deterministic rules (heart rate, SpO₂, blood pressure, sleep) run after each simulation step or when the Medical Officer asks. Each alert shows the rule, readings and threshold used.

🚨

Alerts With an Audit Trail

The Medical Officer acknowledges and resolves alerts and every change is recorded. No diagnosis and no treatment advice.

📡

Offline Operation

After the first successful visit, the app and its locally stored records keep working without a network.

🧾

History & Reports

A safe activity timeline and printable summaries built from the stored synthetic records.

03 · How it works

From reading to response, locally.

🩺

Health Data

Synthetic demo readings and check-ins stored in this browser — no real sensors and no real crew data.

🛰

Local Processing

Deterministic demonstration rules run in the browser, only after a simulation step or when the Medical Officer asks.

🔎

Rule Evaluation

Consecutive readings beyond a stored demonstration threshold open an alert; suspect or stale readings are never used.

⚠

Alerts

Ranked alerts appear consistently in the Medical Officer and astronaut views on this device.

🩹

Human Review

The Medical Officer reviews the evidence and records the outcome. The app does not recommend treatment.

04 · Offline technology

No internet after the first visit.

  • Records are stored locally in IndexedDB, so dashboards keep working without a network.
  • A service worker caches the app's own files after the first successful visit.
  • Demonstration rules run in the browser on stored data; nothing is sent to a server.
  • No external fonts, scripts or analytics are loaded.
