# AstroHealth AI — Data model template

Team 2 fills in this contract before implementing IndexedDB. Team 1 reviews and
approves it before integration. Repositories perform storage operations only;
validation, permissions, risk rules, timeline logging, and events belong in
Team 1 services.

For every store, replace the empty row with its fields, keys, types, required
status, indexes, and notes. Do not store real health information in this student
prototype.
